FAQ
What is the maximum print size?

The current maximum print footprint is about 180 mm x 180 mm. Larger projects may be split into multiple pieces.

How long does international shipping take?

Shipping times vary by country, customs, carrier, and package size. A rough estimate is 7-21 business days after the print is finished.

What file types do you accept?

STL, 3MF, OBJ, STEP, STP, and ZIP files are helpful. For modeling help, photos, sketches, measurements, and reference images are welcome.

Do you offer design or modeling help?

Yes. Printing from an existing file is best, but I can also help create, adjust, resize, or repair a model.
