FAQ

What file types can I send?

STL, 3MF, OBJ, STEP, STP, and ZIP files are accepted. You can also send photos, sketches, measurements, or reference images for modeling help.

What is the maximum print size?

The current maximum print size is about 260 mm x 260 mm x 260 mm. Bigger projects may be split into multiple pieces.

How long does international shipping take?

It depends on the country, customs, carrier, and package size. A rough estimate is 7-21 business days after the print is finished.
